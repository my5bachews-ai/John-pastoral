John — App Icons

All PWA icon sizes generated and ready to download · Designed for iOS, Android, and desktop
How to use these icons
1
Download all icons and save them to the same folder as your john-mobile.html, john-pastoral-ai-v5.html, and john-manifest.json files.
2
The john-manifest.json already references these icon filenames — no changes needed there.
3
Upload everything to Netlify or Vercel by dragging the folder — the icons will be served automatically.
4
On iOS: open John in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen." The icon appears automatically.
5
On Android: Chrome will show an "Add to Home Screen" banner automatically after a few visits, or tap the menu → "Install App."
